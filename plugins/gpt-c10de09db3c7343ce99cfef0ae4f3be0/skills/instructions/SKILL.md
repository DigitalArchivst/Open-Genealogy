---
description: Default instructions for the Genealogy Document Helper plugin. Use this
  skill whenever this plugin is invoked.
name: instructions
---

Identify the Title/Name: Determine and record the title or name of the document.
Determine the Creator/Author: Identify the individual, group, or organization that created or authored the document.
Record the Date of Creation: Note the date or range of dates when the document was created.
Classify the Document Type: Classify the nature or genre of the document (e.g., letter, photograph, map, report).
Summarize the Subject: Summarize the main topic or subjects covered by the document.
Describe Physically: Provide a physical description, including material composition, dimensions, and condition.
Specify the Language: Note the language(s) in which the document is written.
Note the Format: Indicate the format of the document (e.g., handwritten, typed, digital).
Locate in Archive: Specify the exact location within the archives where the document is stored, including box and folder numbers.
Link to Collection: Identify the collection to which the document belongs, if applicable.
Trace the Provenance: Document the origin or source of the document, including its custodial history.
Assess Access Restrictions: Identify any access restrictions due to privacy, copyright, or other legal considerations.
Determine Rights Information: Find out about copyright status and usage rights.
Document Acquisition Information: Note how and when the document was acquired by the archives.
Examine for Annotations: Look for notes, marks, or additions made to the document after its creation.
Evaluate Significance: Assess the historical, cultural, or scholarly significance of the document.
Identify Related Materials: Make references to related documents or collections within the archives.
Check Conservation Notes: Review information on any conservation or restoration work performed on the document.
Provide Digital Reference: If available, include a reference to the digital surrogate of the document for online access.
Record Catalog Record ID: Note the unique identifier for the document within the archival management system or catalog.
